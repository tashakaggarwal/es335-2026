The Question & Unit of Analysis

Research Question
How much probabilistic accuracy (log loss & calibration) does a learned model add over the broadcast heuristic (CRR ≥ RRR), and does it stay calibrated under the 2024–2026 scoring surge?
One Row & Target
State of a second-innings chase at the end of completed over o ∈ {1..19}. Target: chasing_team_won ∈ {0, 1}; output: calibrated p̂ ∈ (0, 1).
Prediction-Time Information
Strictly pre-match history prior to match date, 1st-innings target, deliveries up to over o, and the incoming batter for over o + 1.

Data Source, Size & Quality

Source & Volume
Cricsheet IPL ball-by-ball JSONs (2008–2026): 1,185 regular chases (21,251 2nd-innings over rows; 22,438 1st-innings rows for training DLS). See parse_cricsheet.py.
58 Excluded Matches (match_log.csv)
25 ties/no-results, 23 rain-affected DLS chases, and 10 shortened matches (<20 overs).
Quality Issues Resolved
(1) Scoring inflation: mean target rose from 165.8 to 195.4. (2) Entity drift: 60 venue names unified into 36 grounds; renamed teams mapped. (3) Ball rules: wides/no-balls & retired hurt handled accurately.

Preprocessing Choices & Why

1. Era-Adjusted Target (target_vs_venue_par)
Raw target shifts across eras; subtracting past-only venue par reduces out-of-range test rows to 0.8%.
2. Empirical Exponential DLS Curve
Non-linear dls_resources and par_score_diff fit strictly on 2008–2023 1st innings (dls_params.json) replace linear balls × wickets.
3. Cap RRR & Shrink Small Samples
required_run_rate capped at 36.0; player/venue/form priors shrunk via Empirical Bayes; recency weights (λ = 0.08) applied.

5. Baselines, Models & Validation Selection (Notebook 03 ↗)

Models Compared & Evaluation Protocol

Three baselines and four ML model families were tuned via 5-fold GroupKFold on 2008–2023 and compared on the 2024 validation season (70 matches, 1,239 over states).

Metrics, Bootstrap & Selection Rule

Metrics: Log Loss (Primary), Brier & Accuracy
Log loss rewards calibrated probabilities and penalizes confident errors. Brier score and accuracy (p̂ ≥ 0.5) serve as secondary checks.
Match-Cluster Bootstrap (2,000 Replicates)
Because the 19 overs of a match share one outcome, all 95% CIs and paired comparisons vs. Rule B resample whole matches.
Pre-Registered 0.003 Parsimony Rule
To limit winner’s-curse optimism across 70 validation matches, we pick the simplest candidate (fewest features, linear before trees) within 0.003 log loss of the validation best.

Leakage Controls & Profiling Note

Past-Only History & In-Fold Pipelines
Historical features use matches strictly prior to match date (verified on 60 random matches in NB 02 with 0 disagreements). Median imputation and scaling run inside CV Pipeline folds; trees use early_stopping=False.
Locked Test Set & Honesty Note
Notebooks 01/03 assert season < 2025; NB 04 locks test_results.json after one run. Honesty note: aggregate season target averages were seen during initial profiling, motivating par-normalized features.

Key Validation Findings

Why Linear Beat Boosted Trees & MLP
Once non-linear resource decay is captured by the empirical DLS curve (dls_resources, par_score_diff), the log-odds of winning are nearly linear. Axis-aligned tree splits create step-function jumps and overfit match noise.
Why Extra Groups (D–G) Were Dropped
Adding player, venue, home, and form features (stages D–G) increased variance on 70 validation matches (0.3697 → 0.3866). The 0.003 rule selected the 11-feature B +derived set.
Recalibration Check
5-fold out-of-fold Platt (0.3695) and isotonic (0.3707) recalibration did not improve over raw L2 logistic regression (0.3690).

7. Conclusion, Limitations & Reproducibility

Conclusion, Limitations & How to Reproduce

Conclusion

  • Decisive Improvement Over the Broadcast Rule: On 138 held-out 2025–2026 matches (2,434 overs), the 11-feature L2 logistic regression (B +derived) achieved 0.3815 log loss (0.1219 Brier, 82.05% accuracy) vs. 0.5396 for the CRR ≥ RRR rule—a paired gain of −0.1576 (95% CI [−0.2126, −0.0985], winning 75.4% of matches).
  • Domain Features Beat Complex Models: Encoding non-linear DLS resource depletion (dls_resources, par_score_diff) and venue-par adjustments (target_vs_venue_par) let a simple linear model beat boosted trees (0.3899) and an MLP (0.3856) without post-hoc recalibration.
